# Research log: Skubal

Date: 2026-08-30
Output: `data/research/skubal.csv` (1 row, rejected)
Domain: the claim that started the project

## The claim

Sporting News, and a trail of copy after it, says Tarik Skubal has the perfect name for a
pitcher because Tarik is Arabic for "strike." Skubal has been told this in interviews. The
person is real: two-time AL Cy Young Award, Detroit then Los Angeles.

## What the name means

Tarik is a spelling of Tariq (طارق), the active participle of ṭaraqa (طرق), to knock. Lane,
the Arabic Wikipedia entry and the common reading of Surat al-Tariq all give the same senses:
the one who knocks, a visitor at night, the morning star. The root can also mean to strike or
to beat. That is a real gloss of the verb. It is not a translation of the name, and it is not
a baseball strike.

"Pitcher" does not appear in any of those sources.

## Verdict

Rejected. Score 2: the root is real and the leap is visible, which is how this kind of thing
gets printed. The same standard that kept Derek Jeter (French *jeter*, to throw) would have to
be broken to keep Skubal.

He is in the file anyway. The project exists because this claim was circulating, and the file
exists to say what happened when somebody opened a dictionary.
